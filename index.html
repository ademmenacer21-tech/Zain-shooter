Why it plays well

Built for fast fights

Everything runs in the browser, so you can be in a match seconds after you open the page.

Private rooms

Create a room, get a five-letter code and send it to your friends. Their names appear in your lobby the moment they join.

Select your class

Choose from assault rifles, SMGs, shotguns, a marksman rifle and a sniper, then add a sidearm. Every card shows range, ammo and reload time.

Slide and parkour

Sprint, then slide under fire or into cover. Hop onto crates and barriers, and slide-jump to keep your speed.

A map with height

Climb ramps to the factory roof, the sniper towers and the north catwalk. Fight at ground level or take the high ground.

Controller ready

Plug in a PS5 DualSense or a standard gamepad. Menus are fully navigable and your controller rumbles when you fire and get hit.

No install

Open the page and press Play. Graphics quality, field of view, sensitivity and volume are all adjustable in Settings.

The Yard

Fight on three levels

An industrial compound built around lanes, cover and height. Every position has a counter.

  • Factory roofA central building you can climb from either side. Great sightlines, but you’re exposed.
  • Sniper towers and catwalkTwo raised towers joined by a long catwalk along the north wall for long-range duels.
  • Tanks, containers and barriersTake cover behind storage tanks, shipping containers, crates and jersey barriers. Jump low cover to flank.
  • Ramps, not wallsSlide down a ramp to drop in behind the enemy, or sprint up one to reclaim the high ground.

Questions

FAQ

Is Zain Shooter free?

Yes. It’s free to play and runs entirely in your browser.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open the game page and press Play. A recent version of Chrome, Edge or Firefox with hardware acceleration turned on works best.

How do I play with friends?

In the lobby press Private to create a room, then share the five-letter code or the invite link. Your friends press Join and enter it. When they arrive, their slots fill in and you get a notification.

Why can’t my friend join my room?

Rooms need everyone to open the game page while signed in with access to it. If someone can’t, they can still play solo while you sort out access.

Which controllers are supported?

PS5 DualSense and most standard gamepads your browser recognises. Rumble works. Adaptive triggers aren’t available to web pages.

Can I change the graphics and controls?

Yes. Settings lets you pick Low, Medium or High graphics, and adjust mouse sensitivity, field of view, volume, difficulty and slow auto-regen. Your choices are saved in your browser.
